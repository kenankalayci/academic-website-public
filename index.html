Experimental economist · Brisbane, Australia

Markets, complexity, and consumer decisions

I am a Senior Lecturer in Economics at The University of Queensland. My research uses experiments to study how market complexity, product differentiation, and pricing design influence consumer decisions and competition.

Research areas

Behavioural industrial organization

How consumer biases and limited attention shape firm strategy and market outcomes.

Complexity and obfuscation

How firms use product design, pricing, and information to influence comparison and choice.

Experimental economics

Laboratory and field methods for testing market design and consumer-policy interventions.
